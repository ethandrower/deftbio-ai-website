Why study training breaks

Every site, every role, every amendment

Each study brings its own protocol training, and each site brings its own investigators, coordinators and pharmacists. Every amendment restarts the cycle. Most lean sponsors track it in spreadsheets, chase signatures by email, and rebuild the record before each monitoring visit.

ICH E6(R3) expects training proportionate to each person's role, documented and kept current. Deft makes that the default: training follows the study, the site and the role, and the record builds itself as people sign.
